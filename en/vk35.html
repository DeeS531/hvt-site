High-voltage bushings VK-35 for tank-type circuit breakers 35 kV

High-voltage bushings VK-35 are bushings for tank-type circuit breakers rated 35 kV S-35/VVS-35 VT-35 VM-35 VT-35 MKP-35 VVS-35 VBYET-35, intended to replace both the original bushings supplied by the circuit breaker manufacturers and bushings of the following types: GTV GTPV VPF-35 VKP-35 VL-35 and VVF-35, etc., and are manufactured for currents of 630-1600 A.
