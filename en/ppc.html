PPC-10/630-8 UKHL110 kV, 630 A, oval flange

Polymer lead-through insulator PPC-10/630-8 UKHL1 (oval flange), equivalent of the ceramic insulator IPU-10/630-7,5 UKHL1, IPU 10/630-7,5-I UKHL1, is intended for the entry and insulation of live parts of indoor switchgear of power plants and substations (TP), packaged switchgear (KTP), connection to open switchgear or power lines of 10 kV alternating voltage at frequencies up to 100 Hz, for operation in atmosphere types I or II according to GOST 15150-69, and also for enclosed busducts.

Insulator PPC 10/630-8 UKHL1 is used for repair and equipping of KTP transformer substations with a power of up to 100, 160, 200, 300, 400, 630 kVA.
