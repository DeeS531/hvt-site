Description

OSK insulators

Polymer rod-type post insulators OSK (alternative names IOSK, IOS, IO, ONSH) with a monolithic protective housing replaced the obsolete porcelain insulators IOS, UST, ONSH, ISHT, ONS, AKO more than 12 years ago. They offer higher quality and performance characteristics, reliability, and ease of installation and maintenance.

OSK insulators do not require cleaning of the insulating housing. Owing to their low weight compared with porcelain equivalents they can be installed without crane equipment. They are resistant to vandalism and mechanical impacts (shot, fragments, etc.).

OSK are fully interchangeable with porcelain equivalents, so replacement can be carried out without changing the equipment design. Low weight saves on metal structures in the manufacture of disconnectors and in the construction of foundations for substation equipment.

OSK rod-type post insulators for voltages from 10 to 220 kV have been in operation for more than 20 years at facilities of DTEK Energy, Ukrenergo, Ukrzaliznytsia, and at leading regional power distribution companies of Ukraine. Warranty period — from 5 to 10 years.

The production base has its own electromechanical laboratory: every insulator undergoes mechanical and electrical tests. The company's partners are leading fittings manufacturers in Ukraine, so a complete supply of the necessary materials (clamps, contactors, etc.) is possible.

How to read the designation
O — post-type
S — rod-type
K — silicone rubber (housing material)
4 … 30 — minimum breaking force in bending, kN
10 … 220 — rated voltage, kV
80 … 950 — lightning impulse withstand voltage, kV
1 … 3 — area by atmospheric pollution degree (SZA)
01, 02 … — design version
Example
OSK 8-35/195-3-01: breaking bending force 8 kN, voltage 35 kV, lightning impulse 195 kV, area SZA 3, version 01.
