At the customer's request, OPN 35 surge arresters can be equipped with a surge counter of the type JCQ3, JCQF2, IT-D1, RS-1M and an insulating base.

Surge arresters 35kV can be equipped with the surge counter OPN JCQF3 10/1200 (equivalent of IT-D1, RS-1M, Excount I, Excount II, etc.)

Surge arresters 35kV can be equipped with the surge counter OPN JCQF2 10/800 (equivalent of IT-D1, RS-1M, Excount I, Excount II, etc.)

Surge counters are supplied for the arresters: JCQF2, JCQF3.
