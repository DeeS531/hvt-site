General data

SHOP-35, SHO-35 UKHL1

ParameterValue
Network voltage class, kV35
Full lightning impulse test voltage, kV190
Operating area by atmospheric pollution degree SZAII, III
Creepage distance, mm, not less than1160-1330
Minimum breaking bending force8kN, 10kN, 12,5kN
Type of clamped wiredepends on the busbar support configuration
Code UKT VED8546

The type of clamped wire and other parameters depend on the busbar support configuration.

How to read the designation
SHOP — busbar support with polymer insulation
35 — network voltage class, kV
II, III — atmospheric pollution degree
1, 2 — number of clamped wires
1, 2, 3 — version of the support clamp
01, 02, 03, 04 — support flange configuration
P — suspended version (the support clamp is at the bottom)
R — option of rotating the support flange by 45°
UKHL1 — climatic version and placement category

SHOP-35.II-1 UKHL1Post-type version, 1 wire, SZA II, SZA II

Busbar supports SHOP-35.II-1.1 UKHL1 are intended for fastening, fixing and electrical insulation of live busbars and wires in open and indoor switchgear of substations rated 35 kV. The products are used as part of the busbars of ORU, at transition sections, at connection points of high-voltage equipment, and wherever reliable mechanical support of the conductor is required while maintaining the necessary level of electrical strength.
