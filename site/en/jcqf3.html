Description

JCQF3

The surge counter JCQ3 is intended for continuous indication of the total conduction current and for recording the number of operations of OPN arresters for the 6-330 kV voltage class during overvoltages, as well as for indicating the faulty state of the OPN during operation without breaking the current-carrying circuit.

Operating conditions of the counter JCQ3:

  • ambient temperature from -60 to +60° °C;
  • relative air humidity — not more than 95%;
  • climatic version and placement category per GOST 15150-69 - UKHL1

The surge counter consists of a conduction current indicator with a two-colour scale, an operating-state indicator (green LED), a fault-state indicator (red LED) and an electromechanical operation counter of the OPN.

The surge counter JCQ3 operates as follows. Each operation of the arrester caused by an overvoltage results in a discharge current flowing through the counter. A discharge current above the minimum operating current charges the capacitor. After the discharge current disappears, the capacitor discharges through the windings of the counting mechanism and increases the reading of the counting mechanism by one unit.

Conduction current indicator. During operation the conduction current passes through the surge counter connected in series with OPN. The built-in milliammeter indicates the value of this current. In the dry state of the arrester, the conduction current through OPN must not exceed 2 mA.

Technical specifications of the surge counter JCQ3

  • Network voltage – 10-330 kV
  • Maximum voltage at the instrument terminal of the counter during a lightning impulse – 1,5 kV
  • Rectangular impulse withstand current 2 ms – 1600 A
  • Withstand impulse current 4/10 µs – 100 kA
  • Nominal discharge current (lightning impulse 8/20 µs) - 10 kA
  • Measuring range of AC conduction current – 0-3 mA
  • Monitored number of counter operations – 0-999
  • Mass – 1,1 kg

The counter JCQ3 is an equivalent of the counter JCQF3-S1 and is suitable for replacing counters of the types RS-1, RS-2, RS3, IT-D1.

Surge arresters 35kV can be equipped with the surge counter OPN JCQ3 (equivalent of IT-D1, RS-1M, Excount I, Excount II, etc.)
