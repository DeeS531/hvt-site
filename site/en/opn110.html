Description

OPN-P 110 kV

Surge arresters OPN-P (OPNp) are intended to protect electrical equipment against lightning and switching overvoltages.

How to read the designation
O — arrester
P — overvoltage
N — non-linear
p — polymer, material of the protective ribbed housing — silicone rubber compounds
110 — network voltage class, kV
77 — maximum continuous operating voltage, kV
10 — nominal discharge current, kA
600 — current-carrying capacity current, A
UKHL1 — climatic version and placement category
