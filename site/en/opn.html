Description

OPN-P arresters

The main active part of the OPN consists of a series set of varistors connected in series into a "column". Depending on the required characteristics of the OPN and its design, the arrester may consist of one column or of several columns connected in series or in parallel. The difference between the varistor material of the OPN-P and the resistor material of valve-type arresters is that the non-linear resistors of surge arresters have an increased current-carrying capacity and a highly non-linear current–voltage characteristic (VAKH), which allows the OPN to remain continuously and safely energized while ensuring a high level of protection for electrical equipment. These properties made it possible to eliminate spark gaps from the design of the OPN.

The protective action of a surge arrester is based on the following: when an overvoltage dangerous to the insulation occurs, owing to the high non-linearity of the resistors a considerable impulse current flows through the arrester, as a result of which the overvoltage is reduced to a level safe for the insulation of the protected equipment. In normal operation the current through the arrester is capacitive and amounts to tenths of a milliampere. But when overvoltages occur, the OPNp resistors become conductive and limit any further rise of the overvoltage to a level safe for the insulation of the protected electrical installation. When the overvoltage subsides, the arrester returns to the non-conducting state.

OPN-P surge arresters have passed explosion-safety tests under short-circuit currents according to MEK60099-4, which is important in emergency situations. OPN have been supplied for many years to DTEK, Ukrzaliznytsia, Ukrenergo and other leading power companies of Ukraine.

Advantages of OPNp
  • High hydrophobicity
  • Significantly higher explosion safety than porcelain OPN
  • Vandal resistance
  • Low weight
  • Better electrical and discharge characteristics than OPN with a porcelain housing
  • Ease of installation and transportation, and resistance to shock and vibration
  • Ability to operate in natural and industrial pollution, etc.
