Description

LKC insulators (LK)

Polymer rod-type suspension line insulators LKC with a monolithic protective housing replaced the obsolete skewer-type line insulators LK more than 20 years ago. The protective housing is moulded directly onto the load-bearing unit and joined to it by vulcanization, without sealants. This protects the unit from moisture ingress and prolongs the service life of the insulator.

The insulators are intended for fastening the wires of overhead power lines and for switchgear of AC power plants and substations rated 35–330 kV.

The insulators comply with GOST 28856 and SOU MPE 40.1.51.301:2004; compliance is confirmed by UkrSEPRO certificates.

  • Manufactured according to TU agreed with the Electric Power Department of the Ministry of Fuel and Energy of Ukraine
  • Each insulator undergoes mechanical and electrical tests in the in-house electromechanical laboratory
  • More than 30 years of operation at power utilities in Ukraine and worldwide
  • Minimum order — from 1 piece, manufacturer's price
How to read the designation
L — rod-type suspension line
K — housing material (silicone rubber)
C — monolithic protective housing
70 / 120 / 160 — breaking force in tension, kN
35 … 500 — nominal line voltage, kV
2 … 5 — area by atmospheric pollution degree (SZA)
A, B… — design modification
Example
LKC 120-330-A5: breaking force 120 kN, voltage 330 kV, modification A, for areas up to 5 SZA.
