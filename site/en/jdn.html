Polymer insulators for the railway overhead contact system

Suspension PSKC, tension NSKC, registration FSKC, cantilever KSKC and cantilever-registration KFSKC for 3,3 and 27,5 kV. Models: 14.

Send a request +380 50 947 35 49
Description

Insulators PSKC, NSKC, FSKC, KSKC, KFSKC

The insulators were developed in close cooperation with the Main Directorate of Electrification and Power Supply of Ukrzaliznytsia (now the Power Supply Department); in developing them and launching mass production, all the wishes of the Main Directorate, based on the extensive experience of its specialists in operating such insulators, were taken into account.

Polymer rod-type insulators for the overhead contact system of electrified railways are made at production facilities in Ukraine; the products have certificates of conformity in the UkrSEPRO system.

Railway insulators have more than 18 years of operating experience in the overhead contact system of electrified railways in Ukraine and abroad, and have received many positive reviews from consumers.
