Description

Busbar supports SHOP and SHO

Busbar supports SHOP with polymer insulation and SHO with porcelain insulation are used for fastening, fixing and electrical insulation of live busbars and wires in open and indoor switchgear of substations.

The catalogue contains busbar supports for voltage classes 10, 20, 35, 110, 150, 220 and 330 kV. The version is selected by voltage class, insulation type, mechanical load, wire diameter and grade, mounting dimensions and operating conditions of the site.

Polymer busbar supports have lower weight and are resistant to moisture, pollution and weather. Lower weight reduces the load on the supporting metal structures and simplifies equipment installation.

Need help choosing a busbar support?

We will select a busbar support by voltage class, pollution degree, wire diameter and grade, and mounting dimensions. Delivery across Ukraine by any carrier convenient to you.
