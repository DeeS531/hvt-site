Description

OPN-P 10 kV

The OPN-P-10kV UKHL1 (UKHL2) surge arrester is intended to protect electrical equipment in networks of the 10 kV voltage class, AC, 50 Hz, with isolated or compensated neutral, against lightning and switching overvoltages in areas up to pollution degree V, at an ambient temperature from minus 60°C to plus 60°C, at an altitude of not more than 1000 metres above sea level. Climatic version UKHL, placement category 1 — for outdoor operation.

The OPNp-10kV UKHL1 (UKHL2) arrester is a protective device consisting of columns of series-connected non-linear zinc-oxide (ZnO) varistors and electrodes placed in a polymer housing, together with studs, nuts and washers that allow the arresters to be mounted and connected to the phase and earthing conductors. The arrester contains no spark gaps. The polymer housing is a sealed protective shell moulded from silicone rubber compounds and vulcanized to the surface of the arrester during manufacture. The polymer housing is airtight and moisture-proof.
